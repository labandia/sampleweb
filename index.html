Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended lighting schedule for hydroponic plants?

The recommended lighting schedule is 14 hours ON and 10 hours OFF. This mimics the natural sunlight pattern and gives plants adequate rest periods to prevent issues like tip burn, etc.

How often should I check pH and EC levels?

We recommend checking of pH and EC level after transferring of seedlings and during refilling of water. For lettuce, the ideal EC is 1200-1800 S/cm and pH is 5.5-6.5.

How can I prevent green algae in my hydroponic system?

To prevent green algae:

  1. Block light exposure to water by covering unused holes.
  2. Maintain proper water circulation.
  3. Thoroughly clean the inside and outside plant beds, and make sure to dispose the old water and replace it with fresh, clean water.
  4. Keep water temperature in the optimal range (18-27°C).
What vegetables can I grow with the Hydroponics?

The Hydroponics is ideal for leafy greens like lettuce, spinach, Coriander, Parsley, Rucola, Shiso and herbs. You can also grow smaller varieties of Cherry tomatoes. Refer to seed packaging for specific growing requirements.
